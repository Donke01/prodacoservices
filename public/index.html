Our services

Integrated services for dairy and beef enterprises

Our five service areas support herd productivity, operational control and long-term farm profitability.

Training

Competency-based cattle production training

Programmes range from one-day farm coaching to month-long farm-assistant development. Content is matched to dairy, cow-calf, growing or finishing enterprises.

  • One-day farm coaching: technical assessment and on-site instruction
  • One-week management programme: for owners and supervisors
  • One-month farm-assistant programme: structured practical competency development
  • Farmer Field Schools and training-of-trainers programmes
See training options
Consultancy

Turn farm records into management decisions

We benchmark dairy and beef enterprises, establish performance targets and develop practical management systems for the farm team.

  • Performance benchmarking: milk, growth, fertility, health and cost indicators
  • Farm organisation: SOPs, records, roles and reporting routines
  • Prioritised recommendations linked to commercial objectives
Consultancy services
Cattle Nutrition

Nutrition programmes for every production stage

Forages and concentrates are matched to animal class, liveweight, production stage, available feed resources and performance target.

  • Maize and Boma Rhodes silage
  • Baled Rhodes grass and lucerne hay
  • Dairy concentrates for stage and production level
  • Beef grower and finisher concentrates

Housing & Comfort

Purpose-built cattle housing and handling facilities

We plan dairy housing, cattle shades, beef pens and feedlot layouts around animal comfort, hygiene, workflow and future herd expansion.

  • Dairy free-stall and deep-bedded housing
  • Beef pens, feedlot layouts and cattle-handling flow
  • Ventilation, drainage, shade, feed-bunk and water access
  • Dimensions and bill of quantities for implementation
Shade designs
On-Farm Services

Specialist services delivered on your farm

Our team supports feed conservation, ration formulation, preventive herd-health planning and production monitoring.

  • Silage harvesting: maturity assessment, chopping, compaction and sealing
  • Ration formulation: dairy production, young stock, growth and finishing
  • Herd-health planning: preventive schedules and management protocols
  • Performance monitoring: milk, reproduction, liveweight and feed-use records
