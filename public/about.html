Who we are

About Prodaco Services

A Kenyan animal nutrition and farm services company with one measure of success: your farm producing more.

What we believe

The difference between a struggling farm and a thriving one is rarely the cows — it's feeding, management, housing, and health, done professionally. Those are exactly the things we do.

Prodaco Services brings together animal nutrition expertise and practical farm experience into one company that farmers can call for the things that actually move production: training that sticks, feeds that deliver, designs that work, and services done right the first time.

How we work

  • We come to you. Real advice needs real farms — our work happens where your cows are.
  • We're honest about what pays. If a service won't give you a return, we'll tell you before you spend.
  • We follow up. Our job isn't finished when the invoice is paid — it's finished when the results show.
