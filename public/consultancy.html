An outside expert eye on your farm. We measure, compare, and organise — so you make decisions from facts, not guesswork.

Farm Benchmarking

In one day on your farm, we assess your performance across the areas that drive profit — production, feeding efficiency, herd health, and cost structure — and show you exactly how your farm compares to the best-performing farms. You get a clear picture of your strengths, your gaps, and where the biggest gains are waiting.

Farm Organisation

Many farms underperform not because of the cows, but because of how the farm is run. We help you put structure in place: roles and responsibilities, daily routines, record systems, and workflows — so the farm runs smoothly whether you are there or not.

What you walk away with

  • A clear assessment — where your farm stands today, in plain language and honest numbers.
  • Priorities, not a lecture — the two or three changes that will make the biggest difference first.
  • A practical plan — steps you and your team can actually implement, with our support if you want it.
Benchmarking pairs powerfully with our other services: the assessment tells you what your farm needs — training, better feeds, improved housing, or reorganisation — and we can deliver whichever it is.
