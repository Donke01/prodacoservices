Cow Comfort

A comfortable cow eats more, rests more, stresses less — and pays you back in milk. We design cow shades and housing that work for your herd, your plot, and your budget.

Cow shade designs

Poor housing quietly costs farmers milk every single day — heat stress, wet floors, injuries, and udder infections all trace back to how and where your cows live. Good design prevents all of it, and it doesn't have to be expensive.

Our design service gives you:

  • A site assessment — we visit your farm and design around your actual plot, herd size, and plans for growth.
  • A complete design — layout, dimensions, materials, and orientation done right for ventilation, drainage, and cow flow.
  • A build you can afford — designs matched to your budget, using materials available in your area.
  • Room to grow — designed so you can expand as your herd grows, without demolishing and starting again.
Building or upgrading soon? Talk to us before you pour concrete. Fixing a design on paper costs nothing; fixing a built structure costs a fortune.

Good design pays in

  • 🥛 More milk — comfortable, cool cows produce more
  • 🩺 Fewer health problems — dry floors and clean resting areas
  • ⏱️ Easier daily work — good layout saves hours of labour
  • 💰 Lower building cost — right-sized, no expensive mistakes
